You can now watch a document's bytes get examined, live, and the page still works perfectly with JavaScript turned off.

verify.dmj.one turns credential verification into a visible process: a field of binary 0s and 1s drifts under real force physics, spirals into the verdict while the server re-verifies the post-quantum signature, and only a record that truly passes earns the gold seal strike. Revoked or altered documents fall into red embers; the verdict is server-rendered first, so the truth never waits for the show. Every colour is research-grounded trust psychology: paper-white transparency, institutional blue scrutiny, green confirmation, gold only when it is earned.

The engine is three rendering tiers deep with zero dependencies: WebGPU compute shaders, WebGL2 sprites, then pure CSS, in one ~10KB gzipped file, WCAG 2.2 AAA throughout.
